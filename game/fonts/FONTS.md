# Fonts

All three fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version
1.1, and are vendored here as latin-subset WOFF2 files downloaded from
Google Fonts. They may be freely used, including in this game.

- **Passion One Bold** (`passion-one-bold.woff2`) — display font for
  the logo, big cards, and numbers. Designed by Alfredo Marco Pradil.
- **Barlow Condensed SemiBold** (`barlow-condensed-semibold.woff2`) —
  UI font for HUD text, menus, and the map. Designed by Jeremy Tribby.
- **Yellowtail** (`yellowtail.woff2`) — script font for the tagline
  and district name cards. Designed by Astigmatic (Brian J. Bonislawsky).
